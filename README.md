One line for the **Funniest Autocorrect Fail** competition on [make.fun](https://make.fun/c/u-zeroxfive-funniest-autocorrect-fail-ddf82154): a single autocorrect slip, under 200 characters, judged by an AI comedy editor in head-to-head pairs.

## The entry

> I told my grandma I'd bring the casserole, but autocorrect told her I was bringing the cocaine.

95 characters, 1 line.

## Why this line

- **Clear slip, surprising wrong word:** "casserole" becomes "cocaine" — one letter-cluster away in the keyboard's mind, a world apart in meaning.
- **Reads like a real slip, not a written joke:** the sentence is a plain report of what was texted, exactly how a real autocorrect fail is told.
- **The disaster lands on the last word:** the recipient is Grandma, so the wrong word turns a family dinner into a phone call nobody wants to make.
- **Clear without explanation:** no setup, no punchline label — the joke is the text itself.

node main.ts
